Mindify — Instagram backgrounds
================================

24 files: 8 compositions × 3 formats.

  square   1080 × 1080   feed posts, carousels, quote cards
  portrait 1080 × 1350   taller in the feed; use for posts that matter
  story    1080 × 1920   stories and reel covers

Open preview.html in a browser to see all of them with placeholder copy in
place. That page is the quickest way to choose one — it shows where the text
is meant to sit on each composition.

Where the text goes
-------------------
Each composition leaves one region deliberately quiet. Use it.

  discTop       text bottom-left    disc high right; most headline room
  baseStrata    text top            layers across the bottom third
  quietLines    text centre         nearly empty middle; made for long quotes
  discCentre    text over the disc  highest contrast; keep copy short
  paperStrata   text top, dark type the light one; every 4th or 5th post
  amberBase     text top            warmest; one per grid row at most
  minimal       anywhere            deep and empty; when the words carry it
  ringCorner    text top-right      disc anchored bottom-left

Stories: keep the top 250px and bottom 250px clear — Instagram's own interface
sits there.

Type
----
Headlines in Fraunces (500), tracking about -2.5%. Kickers in Inter semibold,
uppercase, tracking +18%, in amber #D99A3E. Body in Inter regular, #C9D3EA on
dark grounds. All free from Google Fonts.

Colour discipline
-----------------
Amber is an accent. On these backgrounds it appears as a thin base band or a
single dot — never as a large field. If you add graphics of your own, keep to
that. The full palette is in the logo guide.

Editing them
------------
svg/ holds the vector source of every background. They scale to any size and
recolour by search-and-replace on the hex values, so you are never stuck with
these exact crops. The JPEGs are exported at Instagram's native resolution and
93% quality — good enough that Instagram's own recompression won't show.
