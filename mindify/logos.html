Brand · Round 1

Mindify — four logo directions

All four are single-colour, drawn on the same optical grid, and legible down to 16px. Every mark is a live SVG (no raster), so it scales to a billboard and recolours with one CSS variable.

Wordmark: Fraunces (soft serif, warm, slightly humanist). Supporting type: Inter. Both are free and open source — no licensing cost, and they load from Google Fonts.

01 · The Settling Wave Recommended

An agitated line that decays into stillness inside a containing circle. It is literally the window of tolerance — the core idea of Module 2 — and it reads as breath, regulation and calm without ever saying the words.

Why it wins: it's the only concept whose meaning is specific to what you actually teach. Distinctive as a favicon, and the wave alone works as a loading animation, section divider and certificate motif. Risk: at 16px the oscillation flattens — the small-size version drops to three peaks.

02 · The Open Loop

Two nested arcs — an inhale and an exhale — that deliberately never close, with a single dot resting in the gap. Breath work, mindfulness, and the ‘good enough’ principle: the circle doesn't have to be complete to hold.

Why it might win: the most conventionally beautiful of the four and the most obviously "mindfulness/wellbeing" — good if you plan to expand beyond parenting into breathwork and self-paced psychology generally. Risk: open-ring marks are a crowded category. Lower distinctiveness.

03 · The Secure Base

Three nested arcs cradling a single filled dot — a harbour, a shelter, a held child. Directly Bowlby: a secure base to explore from and a safe haven to return to.

Why it might win: the warmest and most obviously about a child — strongest emotional hit with parents and carers. Also reads as sound waves or a signal, which flatters the ‘listening to behaviour’ idea. Risk: narrows the brand to parenting. Harder to stretch to adult self-development courses later.

04 · The M Monogram

An M built from two arches of unequal weight — two people, or two breaths, one leaning on the other — topped with a small dot. The most corporate and the most flexible of the four.

Why it might win: a letterform mark survives everything — app icon, embroidered polo shirt, single-colour fax. Best choice if Mindify becomes a platform with many course brands under it. Risk: it says nothing about psychology. It's a container, not a story.
