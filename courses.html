The curriculum

Six modules, one thread

Each module builds on the last. Full lesson content unlocks in your student area once you're enrolled.

Module 1 · 120 min

Foundations — From Control to Connection

“Behaviour is communication. Connection comes before correction.”

Locate your own default parenting stance without judgement, then meet the single reframe the whole course rests on: behaviour is the tip of an iceberg, and the interesting part sits below the waterline.

  • Tell control-based, permissive and therapeutic parenting apart
  • Explain why connection has to come before correction
  • Use the iceberg to find the need beneath a behaviour
The judge vs. the detectiveThe smoke alarm Details
Module 2 · 120 min

The Developing Brain & Nervous System

“You are parenting a brain and a body still under construction.”

Why ‘just calm down’ asks for something a child's brain may not have built yet. The hand model, flipping your lid, and the window of tolerance — plus the response sequence that actually lands.

  • Use the hand model to explain the upstairs and downstairs brain
  • Explain why the thinking brain comes online last
  • Recognise the window of tolerance and hyper/hypo-arousal
Siegel's hand modelRegulate → Relate → Reason Details
Module 3 · 120 min

Attachment & the PACE Way of Being

“Be the secure base. Parent with PACE.”

Attachment as a launchpad and a harbour. Why a child's coping patterns are clever survival strategies rather than flaws — and how the PACE stance deepens felt safety in ordinary exchanges.

  • Explain attachment as a secure base and a safe haven
  • See coping patterns as survival strategies, not labels
  • Describe the PACE stance and why it builds felt safety
Bowlby & AinsworthServe and return Details
Module 4 · 120 min

Co-Regulation & Big Feelings

“Children borrow our calm. Regulate before you educate.”

Self-regulation is built from thousands of experiences of being co-regulated. Learn the shape of the wave, why we never teach at the peak, and a five-step ladder you can climb in a real moment of overwhelm.

  • Explain co-regulation and why it precedes self-regulation
  • Tell a tantrum from a meltdown — and respond to each
  • Use a five-step regulation ladder in a live moment
Borrowing calmThe wave Details
Module 5 · 120 min

The Reason, Not the Behaviour

“Every behaviour is a message. Respond to the need, not just the act.”

The hidden reasons behind the behaviours that test you most, why sticker charts and punishments so often disappoint with a dysregulated child, and how to design a therapeutic response that keeps the boundary intact.

  • Read behaviour as communication of an unmet need
  • Tell natural and logical consequences apart from punishment
  • Explain why reward charts fail a dysregulated child
Hidden reasonsConsequences vs. punishment Details
Module 6 · 120 min

Rupture, Repair & the Long Game

“Repair is where connection is rebuilt. Sustain the parent to sustain the parenting.”

The most freeing idea in the course: rupture is normal and it isn't what wounds. Learn the repair sequence, the shame/guilt distinction that protects a child from the inside out, and how to keep going through setbacks.

  • Repair a rupture skilfully — own your part and reconnect
  • Tell shame from guilt and reduce toxic shame
  • Sustain the change through setbacks; embrace ‘good enough’
